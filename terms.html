Terms of Use

Orange Pi Backups is a personal utility for backing up a privately operated Minecraft server to Google Drive storage authorized by its owner. It is not a public hosting or file-sharing service.

Only accounts and files that the operator is authorized to access may be used. The account owner controls OAuth authorization, cloud storage and deletion of stored backups.

The utility’s backup process and data handling are described on the home page and in its Privacy Policy. Storage use remains subject to the applicable Google Drive terms.

For questions, contact the operator through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
